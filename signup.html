VERIFYX · LEGAL

Terms and Conditions

These sample terms describe intended use of the VERIFYX prototype. They must be reviewed and approved by West Visayas State University and its Data Protection Officer before production deployment.

1. Acceptance and purpose

VERIFYX is intended to support attendance recording and examination identity verification for authorized university activities.

2. Biometric information

Facial images and biometric templates may constitute sensitive personal information. Any collection or processing must have a valid legal basis, clear notice, appropriate safeguards, and institutional approval.

3. Verification and attendance

Camera-based face detection alone does not establish identity. Automated results must be subject to a properly validated recognition process and appropriate human review.

4. Examination verification

Uncertain or unsuccessful matches must be referred for manual identity checking. A failed match alone must not be treated as proof of misconduct.

5. Privacy and security

Processing must comply with applicable Philippine laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules, and relevant institutional policies.

6. Authorized use and responsibilities

Users must protect their credentials and use the system only for authorized university purposes. Administrators must follow role-based access and data-handling policies.

7. Retention and account management

Retention, access, correction, deletion, and account-closure procedures must be defined by the university before production use.

VERIFYX · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

This prototype uses browser camera access only after the user selects Start Camera. The current demo does not transmit camera frames to a VERIFYX server, create biometric templates, or perform identity matching. Production use requires a university-approved privacy notice, lawful processing basis, security controls, retention policy, and contact details for the responsible office.
